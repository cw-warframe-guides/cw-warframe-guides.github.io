## Overview

With the Icebind and Riven changes in 2026, the old system of simply hitting reroll until you get a Riven you like is no longer the only strategy. Spliced traits and the ability to lock stats allow you to significantly reduce the amount of RNG needed to get good and god-tier Rivens. 

---
## What Stats to Aim For
While there's no set list of 'best' traits, here's my list of traits to look at when figuring out what stats you want to aim for. 

### Great Stats
- Critical Damage
- Multishot
- Status Damage
- Gas

### Good Stats
- Blast
- Corrosive / Viral
- Weakpoint Damage
- Fire Rate
- Critical Chance
- Damage
- Ammo Efficiency
- Slam Damage

### Unique Conditional Stats
- Reload While Holstered
- Parry Angle

Additionally, this resource also exists which covers ideal rolls for every Riven. [Good Rolls for Selling](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1zbaeJBuBn44cbVKzJins_E3hTDpnmvOk8heYN-G8yy8/edit?gid=1687910063#gid=1687910063)

## The Rolling Strategy

This locking strategy assumes you want a spliced trait and know what traits you want. If you just want non-spliced traits, skip steps 3 & 4. 

1. First, roll for your ideal configuration. In most cases this'll be a 3p1n.
2. Lock a trait to keep the config. If you're aiming for Slam Damage, Status Damage on melees, or any elemental spliced traits, lock the negative; otherwise, lock a random positive.
3. Roll until you get one of the traits for your splice, then lock it. At this stage, if grades matter, keep rolling until you reach a good grade.
4. Roll until you get the second trait, then splice.
5. Roll until you get the second stat you want, at a grade you want, and lock it.
6. Just donkey roll until you get the final two stats you're happy with.
